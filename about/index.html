About Privesy

The easiest way to create privacy policies for your apps.

Privesy helps independent developers and small teams publish clear, store-ready privacy documents without a legal retainer.

What is Privesy?

Privesy is a free, open-source tool designed to help app developers quickly generate professional privacy policies and terms of service. Whether you are publishing on Google Play, Apple App Store, or any other platform, Privesy makes compliance easy.

Features

Quick & Easy

Generate a complete privacy policy in under 2 minutes.

AI-Powered

Use Gemini or ChatGPT to generate custom clauses.

Free Hosting

Host your policy with a shareable link instantly.

App Store Ready

Compliant with Google Play & App Store requirements.

Cookie Consent

Generate GDPR-compliant cookie banners for your website.

How we build Privesy

Privacy by default

API keys stay in your browser. We only store the policy text and contact details you explicitly host.

Transparent templates

The generated text is plain Markdown, so you can read, compare and edit every section.

No lock-in

Download your documents at any time. Hosting is optional and there is no subscription.
