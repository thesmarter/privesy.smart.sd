What is Privesy?

Privesy is a free privacy policy generator for mobile and web apps. It creates a structured privacy policy and terms of service from your app details, then lets you download the documents or host them on a shareable public URL. No account is required, and optional AI features use your own Gemini or OpenAI key.

  • Templates cover data collection, third-party services, children’s privacy and contact details.
  • Generated documents are editable Markdown, so you can adapt every clause.
  • Hosted policies can be updated later from your dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about generating and hosting privacy policies with Privesy.

What is a privacy policy and why do I need one?

A privacy policy explains what data your app collects and how it is used. Most app stores and privacy laws require one before you can publish your app.

Is Privesy free to use?

Yes, generating and previewing policies is free. You only need to bring your own AI key if you want to use the optional AI assistant.

Does the generated policy meet Google Play and App Store requirements?

The generator covers the sections stores commonly ask for, such as collected data, third-party services, and contact details. You should still review the text to make sure it matches your app’s actual behavior.

Do I need an AI (Gemini/OpenAI) key?

No, the key is optional and only powers the AI assistant that improves wording. Your key stays in your browser and is never sent to our servers.

How does hosting and sharing work?

You can publish your policy to a public link on privesy.smart.sd and use that URL in your store listing. You can edit and re-publish your policy at any time from your dashboard.

Can I also generate a cookie banner?

Yes, the cookie banner tool creates a customizable consent banner for your website. Just configure the message, copy the snippet, and paste it into your site’s HTML.
