Play Data Safety Helper

Answer four quick questions to draft your Google Play Data safety answers. Copy the summary into the Play Console Data safety form.

The Google Play Data Safety section must match what your app actually does. Use this helper to write clear, consistent answers you can copy into the Play Console.

Play Data Safety Helper

Personal data includes names, emails, device IDs, or any info that identifies a user. If checked, declare each data type and its purpose in the Play Console Data safety form.

Sharing covers SDKs, analytics, ads, or backends that receive user data. List every recipient, e.g. AdMob, Firebase, OneSignal, and link their privacy policies.

Google expects HTTPS/TLS for all network transfers. If unchecked, enable TLS on every endpoint before submitting the Data safety form.

Play policy requires a deletion mechanism or contact path. Provide an in-app option or a support email, and mirror it in your privacy policy.

How to use this helper

  1. Select the statements that are true for your app.
  2. Review the generated summary and adjust any answer that does not match your app.
  3. Copy the summary for your records or share it with your team.
